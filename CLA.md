## How to sign

When You open Your first pull request, a check named `cla` asks You to sign.
Post this comment on the pull request, exactly as written:

```
I have read the Spero CLA v1.0 and I hereby sign it.
```

The check then turns green. Your signature is the comment itself, tied to Your
GitHub account, and it covers all of Your later pull requests to Spero.
